Chicken Enchiladas

Description:

A delicious Mexican-inspired dish made with seasoned chicken, tortillas, enchilada sauce, and melted cheese.

Baked until warm, bubbly, and golden on top.

Ingredients:

  • Chicken breast
  • Corn or flour tortillas
  • Enchilada sauce
  • Shredded cheddar cheese
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Olive oil
  • Cumin
  • Chili powder
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Fresh cilantro

Steps

  1. Cook and shred the chicken.
  2. Sauté the onion and garlic until softened.
  3. Season the chicken with cumin, chili powder, salt, and black pepper.
  4. Add some enchilada sauce to the chicken mixture.
  5. Fill each tortilla with the chicken mixture and roll it tightly.
  6. Place the rolled tortillas in a baking dish.
  7. Pour enchilada sauce over the top.
  8. Sprinkle shredded cheese evenly over the enchiladas.
  9. Bake until the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  10. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve warm.
