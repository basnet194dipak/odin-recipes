Chicken Noodle Casserole

Description:

A comforting baked casserole made with tender chicken, egg noodles, creamy sauce, and vegetables.

Topped with cheese and baked until hot, creamy, and golden.

Ingredients:

  • Chicken breast
  • Egg noodles
  • Cream of chicken soup
  • Milk
  • Shredded cheddar cheese
  • Frozen peas and carrots
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Butter
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • Breadcrumbs

Steps:

  1. Cook the egg noodles according to the package instructions.
  2. Cook and shred the chicken into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Sauté the onion and garlic in butter until softened.
  4. Mix the cream of chicken soup, milk, and seasonings in a large bowl.
  5. Add the cooked noodles, chicken, peas, and carrots.
  6. Stir everything together until evenly combined.
  7. Transfer the mixture to a greased baking dish.
  8. Sprinkle shredded cheese and breadcrumbs over the top.
  9. Bake until the casserole is hot and the topping is golden.
  10. Let it cool slightly before serving.
